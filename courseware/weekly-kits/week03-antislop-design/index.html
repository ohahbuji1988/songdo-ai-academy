# DESIGN SYSTEM CONSTRAINTS
- Palette: Max 2 colors. Dominant: Slate (#0f172a, #334155), Accent: Teal/Emerald (#0f766e, #00c471).
- Typography: Wanted Sans Variable, letter-spacing: -0.015em.
- Korean Rule: Always word-break: keep-all; text-wrap: balance; (No orphan syllables like '을/를').
- No Slop: Never use purple/pink gradients, rainbow borders, or cheesy AI glow effects.
- Spacing: Consistent 16px/24px/32px grid paddings with subtle 1px border (#e2e8f0).
